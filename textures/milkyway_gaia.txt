milkyway_gaia.png - Milky Way texture of the photographic mode
==============================================================

Source
------
Derived from the Milky Way skybox (six cubemap faces) of the Gaia Sky
high-resolution texture pack, version 15 of 2026-01-09:
  https://gaia.ari.uni-heidelberg.de/gaiasky/repository/tex/v015_20260109/
Gaia Sky: Toni Sagrista Selles, Astronomisches Rechen-Institut (ARI/ZAH),
Heidelberg University - https://gaiasky.space
The Milky Way of Gaia Sky is based on data of the ESA mission Gaia
(ESA/Gaia/DPAC).

Processing (for Stellarium)
---------------------------
- the six cube faces re-projected to Stellarium's equatorial layout
  (4096 x 2048, column 0.25 - RA/360, top row Dec +90),
- adaptive smoothing of the faint, quantized regions; the darkened spots at
  the galactic poles of the source corrected with a cosecant law,
- treated as linear in surface brightness and calibrated to the mean
  integrated starlight at the zenith at 40 N (Masana et al. 2021, MNRAS 501,
  5443) minus the light of the stars Stellarium draws itself
  (see src/core/modules/MilkyWay.cpp),
- stored sRGB-encoded (the GPU decodes it back to linear light).
- the source image is stretched for display (its contrast compressed), so
  the photographic mode expands it again when drawing: the luminance is
  raised to the power 1.5 (config: astro/milky_way_photo_destretch), with
  the calibration kept at the same zenith mean (1.0 = 1400 S10(V)). The
  star clouds of Sagittarius and Scutum then reach about 20 mag/arcsec^2.
